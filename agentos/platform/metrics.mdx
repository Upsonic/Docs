AgentOS counts everything that passes through it, so there is nothing to instrument in your agent. Every run, hold and model call made through the gateway is counted.

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the environment's front page: executions, tokens, cost and outcomes across every agent, and a card per live agent (the denser its flow line, the busier the agent).

Pick a range in the top right:

| Range | Covers | Each bar is |
|---|---|---|
| **D** | the last 24 hours | an hour |
| **M** | the last 30 days | a day |
| **Y** | the last 12 months | a month |

What it shows:

- **Executions.** Every run counts, and a child run counts as an execution of its own. The throughput chart stacks each bucket's failed runs (red) on top of the others (green), on the same scale.
- **How executions ended.**
  - **completed on their own**: finished without asking anyone,
  - **went to a person**: asked a person at least once, whatever happened next,
  - **failed or cancelled**.

  A held run is not a failure: its agent asked a person to decide.
- **Waiting for a person now**: the holds open at this moment. Decide them in the [Review queue](/agentos/platform/review-queue).
- **Tokens** and **Cost** of the model calls. Costs come from the gateway's price table for each model.
- Changes against the previous period of the same length.

## An agent's Overview

The agent's **Overview** tab shows the same numbers for one agent, with the same D / M / Y ranges:

- **Executions** (and their rate per hour, day or month), **Tokens** (per run), **Cost**, and **Held for a person**, with how long the oldest one has been waiting.
- **Executions and latency**: throughput, with p50 and p95 run duration. A run's duration includes the time it waited for a review. The latency line breaks where a bucket had no runs.
- **How runs ended**, as on the Dashboard.
- **What went wrong**: failed runs grouped by error code. Guardrail blocks are also grouped by their message.
- **Recent runs**, **Versions**, and the **Declared configuration** and **Capabilities** of the selected version, from its `agentos.yaml`.

## Models page usage

The **Models** page shows each model's calls, tokens and cost over the last 30 days. A model that cost nothing is marked **on-prem**. See [Models and providers](/agentos/platform/models-and-providers).

## How the numbers are counted

- **Cost.** A model the price table doesn't know costs $0. A cost under a cent shows as `<$0.01`. "On-prem models cost $0" is shown only when every model called in the range cost nothing.
- **Eval runs** are not counted anywhere on these pages, and an AI judge's tokens appear on its experiment, not on the Models page. See [Evals](/agentos/platform/evals).
- **Times are UTC.**

For one run's own tokens, cost and model calls, open the run: its `usage` and timeline list every call. See [Runs API](/agentos/api/runs).
